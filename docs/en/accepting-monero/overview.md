## Confirmation times

Exchanges and payment processors typically wait for **10 confirmations** before crediting Monero deposits. At Monero's ~2-minute block time, this means roughly 20 minutes of settlement time.

For a live reference comparing Monero's confirmation time against other chains, see [OpenChainBench L1 Finality](https://openchainbench.com/benchmarks/l1-finality), which measures wall-clock finality across Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Monero and more, updated every 10 seconds.
